It have been a long time, maybe it s the first time I write a blog like this. 

First of all, my English skill are terrible. So dont be judge here. I am done my disclaimer.

Now back to today, after a week in Phu Quy Island, I really love this place. You know the first day in here, I cant find a "normal" food -- a local eatery, I used gg translate for this word. A slightly think through my mine: "Oh hoo lee sheet, I'm going to dead in this island". After running with my motobike - rent from homestay where I stay - about an hour, I almost saw all spots where people "take a picture" when come this island BUT no food :DDD. I GO OUT FOR FOOD, NOT PICTURE ~~yet~~. When finnaly I foud some local eateries in "Cong chua Ban Tranh" street, I almost staved. Then I know a fact that this time is not a good time to travel in this island then no travler, no visitor, no service -- no service mean a rare service are open this time. 

But this is the first day. The Owner of homestay suggested me to visit [Ong 12](https://maps.app.goo.gl/AoutHp1P3zw89kGk6?g_st=ic) - a local eatery and its really a good place. Nice `Bún bò`, nice `Cơm sườn`. But closed early. And thank God, I found another local eatery [Izakaza Hasuda](https://maps.app.goo.gl/DtpKCP2WHEQxzefJA?g_st=ic), a cozy place with good `cơm lươn`.
